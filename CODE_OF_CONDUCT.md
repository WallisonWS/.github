# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our open-source community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
* Focusing on what is best for the open-source community and users.
